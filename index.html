Passkey P-256 Signing

Create a passkey (ES256 / P-256), then use it to sign a payload. The signature is verified right here in the browser with WebCrypto. Nothing leaves your device.

Passkeys known to this page

    Only the public keys are stored (in this browser's localStorage). Forgetting them here does not delete the passkey from your device.

    2. Sign a payload

    How it works

    1. The page computes challenge = SHA-256(payload).
    2. The authenticator signs authenticatorData || SHA-256(clientDataJSON) with ECDSA P-256, where clientDataJSON contains the challenge.
    3. A verifier checks the challenge matches the payload hash, then checks the signature against the public key saved at registration.
